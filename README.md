# Marriott International — Statistical Methods Market Research Proposal

## Overview

This project develops a **market research proposal for Marriott International** to investigate customer behaviour, travel trends, loyalty, satisfaction, and sustainability.

The project was structured as a simulated market research agency engagement, with the objective of translating Marriott's business requirements into a structured research and analytical framework.

## Research Aims

The research focuses on four key areas:

1. **Customer Preferences**
   Understanding preferences for personalised services and technological integration.

2. **Travel Trends**
   Identifying emerging travel trends, destination preferences, and differences across demographic groups.

3. **Customer Loyalty & Satisfaction**
   Evaluating loyalty programme effectiveness and guest satisfaction across Marriott brands and regions.

4. **Sustainability & Economic Factors**
   Investigating how sustainability initiatives influence customer loyalty and how economic factors affect travel behaviour.

## Methodology

The research design includes:

* Research questions and objectives
* Sampling methodology
* Questionnaire design
* Data collection strategy
* Statistical analysis framework
* Focus Group Discussions (FGDs)
* Project timeline and Gantt chart
* Research budget

The proposed quantitative and qualitative approaches were designed to generate insights that could support Marriott's understanding of customer behaviour and market opportunities.
